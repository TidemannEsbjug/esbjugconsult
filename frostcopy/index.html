Common questions.

Can a copy be traced back to people?
Yes. Whoever has the key can turn the codes back into names. Choose “Labels only” or “Black redaction” when the copy must never be restorable, and always read it through before you share it.
Can others who have FrostCopy turn my codes back?
No. The codes can only be turned back with your key. Others have their own key, and with it your codes are just codes. If you want someone to be able to restore your copies, you share the key with them.
What if I lose the key?
Then no one can turn the codes back into names. Your originals are untouched. That is why FrostCopy asks you to keep the key safe, as 24 words on paper or a key file.
Does FrostCopy need the internet?
No. FrostCopy never connects to the internet, and works just as well offline.
Will it run on my computer?
Probably. FrostCopy runs on ordinary work computers, with no graphics card. Check your computer
Can a colleague restore a copy I made?
Yes, when you have the same key, or when the colleague has added yours.
Does FrostCopy learn from me?
Yes, from what you do. If you mark a name, a nickname or a word FrostCopy missed, FrostCopy remembers it and hides it in everything it reads afterward. The list is called “Always hide”. It is stored encrypted on your computer, and you can take things out of it. Nothing is sent to us, and your documents are not used to train any model.
What if FrostCopy misses something?
It can happen. That is why you look over what FrostCopy finds, and FrostCopy checks every copy before it is made. See what FrostCopy catches, and what it does not
What happens to the originals?
Nothing. FrostCopy only writes new files, in a separate folder next to them.

What FrostCopy looks for

You choose what the scan looks for. The ten groups that are on from the start are listed under This is what FrostCopy looks for.

You turn these on when you need them: Dates, Employer (organization and company IDs) and Case numbers.

Social Security numbers and EINs have no check digit, so FrostCopy goes by how they are written and by labels and column headers. Card numbers, routing numbers, NPIs and VINs are checked against their check digits, so a random number of the same length is not taken for one of them.

Your office's own details are kept: the address, the main phone number, the EIN and the operating account.

The files FrostCopy reads, and how the copy turns out
Word, Excel and PowerPoint
The same file, with the codes where the information was. Layout, logos and tables stay as they were. Comments, macros, embedded files and hidden information in images are removed.
PDF, including scans
Each page becomes an image with the codes painted where the information was. Underneath is an invisible text layer, so you can still search the copy.
Images
FrostCopy reads the text in the image. Information is painted over, and faces are pixelated.
Email
Reads .eml, Outlook (.msg), Apple Mail (.emlx) and .mbox. The copy becomes an .eml. Attachments are taken out, and the copy says so.
Text
.txt, .md, .csv, .json and .rtf.

File and folder names are cleaned too. The copies are placed in a new folder next to the original, with “-clean” after the name, together with a report.

Old .ppt files cannot be read, and FrostCopy tells you about them. Password-protected PDF and Office files are opened when you enter their password.

Rules first
US patterns, check digits, name lists and addresses. The rules go through every file.
Then a small English language model
It is based on distilbert-base-cased and fine-tuned for FrostCopy. It judges whether a sentence says something private about a person. It needs no graphics card.
Text in images
Scanned pages and images are read with text recognition on the computer.
Faces
FrostCopy finds faces and pixelates them. You can keep a face.

FrostCopy has no chatbot, and sends nothing to any AI service.

What is in the package

FrostCopy is an ordinary desktop program. The shell is written in Rust (Tauri), and the window is a web view with an interface in TypeScript. Everything it needs is in the installer. Nothing is downloaded afterward.

Rule engine
TypeScript. Runs in the program, with no server.
Sentence model
Based on distilbert-base-cased by Hugging Face (Apache 2.0), fine-tuned for FrostCopy. It runs on the processor with tract, in pure Rust.
Text in images
Apple Vision on Mac. Windows' own text recognition and Tesseract with English on Windows.
Faces
Apple Vision on Mac. Small face models (YuNet and UltraFace) on Windows.
PDF
PDFium draws the pages and reads the text layer.
No large language model
FrostCopy has no LLM and no chatbot. The build stops if one is in the package.

From file to finding

Each file is unpacked into pieces of text that know where they are: paragraph, cell, text box, header or a place on a PDF page. Then three readers go over the text.

The rules
Social Security numbers, EINs and driver's license numbers have no check digit, so FrostCopy goes by the written form and by labels and column headers. Card numbers (Luhn), routing numbers, NPIs and VINs must have the right check digits. IBAN has its own check. Names are found with name lists and by where they stand, such as after “Name:” or in a signature.
Sensitive words
A word list for health, immigration, criminal history, religion and union membership, with the forms each word takes. Different forms of the same word count as the same hit.
The sentence reader
The language model gives each sentence about a person a number for how private it is. Together with a word-based reader, that decides whether the sentence becomes a “Note”.
Your office's own
The address in the letterhead, the main phone number, the EIN and the operating account are recognized and kept. Email addresses are hidden anyway.

Finally the findings are gathered in the folder's register. “Karen”, “Karen Nelson” and “KAREN NELSON” become one person, in all the files. What FrostCopy is not sure about goes in the “Unresolved” queue with the reason: the name is first in a sentence, two people have the same name, or the word was added afterward.

The codes

A code has two parts. One says who it is. The other is the text as it was, encrypted.

[PERSON_VBTF60YD0E-J94DW5ZFTYA2GWTWAXFKK0XYBF1KTY19BED6E5TJ832VZ00]

The person code
HMAC-SHA256 of the type and the value, shortened to ten characters. The same for every spelling of the same name, so an AI tool sees that it is the same person.
The encrypted part
AES-SIV (RFC 5297) of the text exactly as it was. The same text always gives the same code. A code that has been changed, or made with another key, is rejected. FrostCopy never restores to something wrong.
The short number
The number in [Person 4829133] is computed from the key and the value. That is why it is the same on all computers with the same key, without them talking to each other.
The code list
In the usual copy format, the short number is in the text and the full codes are in a list at the end. The list is all that is needed to restore the copy, together with the key.

The key

What it is
32 random bytes, made on the computer the first time FrostCopy starts. Subkeys for codes, numbers and images are derived with HKDF-SHA256.
Where it is kept
Protected by the operating system: the keychain and Secure Enclave on Mac, DPAPI on Windows. The key is held in the Rust part of the program. The window only gets it when you ask to see the 24 words.
24 words
The key can be written down as 24 words from a list of 2,048. The words are Norwegian, use only the letters a to z, and no two look alike. The first four letters are enough to recognize a word.
Key file
The key encrypted with a passphrase of at least twelve characters (Argon2id and AES-256-GCM).
Key ID
A short identifier such as WJ0R-Q3R5. It says which key a copy was made with. It cannot be used as the key.
Several keys
If you change keys, the old one is set aside. When restoring, FrostCopy tries the key in use first, then the earlier ones.

How the copy is written

Word, Excel, PowerPoint
The file is a zip with XML. FrostCopy replaces the values where they are, also when Word has split a name into several pieces. Then the file is cleaned: comments, macros, embedded files, the preview and hidden information in the images are taken out.
PDF
Each page is drawn as an image at 200 dots per inch, 300 when the type is small. The codes are painted where the information was, and the cleaned text is placed invisibly underneath, so the copy can be searched.
Images
The boxes are painted over, and the faces are pixelated.
What was underneath
When the copy should be restorable, FrostCopy stores the original pixels under each box encrypted in the copy (AES-256-GCM). Ordinary programs skip them.
Email
The copy becomes a tidied .eml. Technical lines about the route the email took are removed.
The file names
If there is a name in the file or folder name, it is replaced too.

The check before the copy is made

Before a copy is written, FrostCopy reads it the way another program would: every part of the zip file, every XML attribute, every object in the PDF and the text in every image. If it finds any of what was to be hidden, or a part it cannot read, the file is not made. You get to see what it concerns.

What FrostCopy stores

The project
What you have decided in a folder is stored encrypted on the computer, and kept out of Spotlight and Time Machine. A project that is not used is forgotten after 30 days.
The backup
A copy of the originals. The content is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and can only be read on the computer it was made on. The file names are left as they are.
Your marks
The list of what you have asked FrostCopy to hide is stored encrypted.
The originals
FrostCopy only writes to folders it has made itself. Every place in the code that deletes or moves a file must have a written justification. Otherwise a test stops the build.

What it does not have

The program has no TLS, cannot look up a name on the network and does not listen on any port. It has no account, no usage statistics, no error reporting and no automatic updates. The license is a signed code (Ed25519) that is checked on the computer. The only thing FrostCopy does toward the network is open our website in your own browser when you press “Buy a license”.
