Privacy

The information stays with you.

FrostCopy is made to keep personal information from going astray. That applies to yours too. Here is what happens in the program, on the website and when you buy.

In short

  • The program sends nothing. Your documents never leave the computer, and we never see them.
  • The website does not track you. The FrostCopy pages set no cookies, and have no ads and no analytics tools from others.
  • If you buy, we store what is needed to deliver. The organization, the email address and the payment.

The program

FrostCopy cannot connect to the internet. The program has no account, no usage statistics, no error reports and no automatic updates. There is nothing we can collect.

Your documents
They are read and cleaned on your computer. We never receive them, and we do not handle them for you. For the documents, you or your organization are responsible.
What FrostCopy remembers
What you have decided in a folder, your own marks, backups and the key are on your computer, encrypted. You can delete them whenever you want.
The license
The license code is checked on the computer. The program does not call home to ask whether it is valid.
When the program opens the website
If you press “Buy a license”, our website opens in your browser. The address includes a machine code, which identifies the installation. It says nothing about your documents.

The IT department can check this themselves. See the checks on the page for IT.

The website

The FrostCopy pages are at esbjugconsult.com/frostcopy. They load fonts and images from ourselves, and nothing from other websites.

Cookies
The FrostCopy pages set none.
Counting visits
We count visits to see whether the pages are used. For each visit we store the time, which page it was, the city and the country (estimated from the IP address), and whether it was a phone or a computer. The IP address is not stored.
A new visit, or someone who has been here before
To tell the two apart we make a code from the network you come from. The code cannot be computed back to the IP address. It is stored together with the city and the time. If you have said yes to cookies on the front page of esbjugconsult.com, that cookie is used in place of the network.
The price calculator
What you enter on the price page, the number of people and the kind of business, is calculated in your browser. It is not sent to us.
The computer check
What you type in “Will FrostCopy run on your computer?” is looked up in our own list. The search is not stored.
Download
We do not count downloads, and we do not link them to an organization.
Errors on our side
When something goes wrong, we log the error.

The website is hosted at Cloudflare. As with all websites, whoever delivers the page has to see your IP address to be able to send the page to you.

When you buy

FrostCopy is free for private individuals, and then we need nothing from you. Buying online is not open yet. An organization that buys a license gives us this:

The organization
The name, the number of people and the kind of business. The price is calculated from them.
Email addresses
The address the invoice goes to, and the address the license key goes to if it is a different one.
The license key
The key contains the organization's name, the email address of the person who bought it, and the dates the license is valid. Whoever gets the key can read this.
Questions to us
If you send us a question by email, we keep the name, the email address and the message, so we can answer.

This section will be updated when buying online opens, with who handles the payment.

Who else gets something

We do not sell information, and we do not give it to anyone for advertising. This company helps us deliver:

Cloudflare
Hosts the website and stores what is described on this page. Cloudflare is an American company.

Why we are allowed to

Esbjug Consult is a Norwegian company, so the European data protection rules (GDPR) apply to what we do with this information, also when you are in the United States.

The agreement with you
What we need to deliver the license (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
The law
We have to keep the accounts (Article 6(1)(c)).
Legitimate interest
Counting visits, answering questions and logging errors (Article 6(1)(f)). We have weighed this against your privacy, and for that reason we do not store the IP address.

How long

Licenses
As long as the license is valid.
Accounts
As long as the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act requires.
Questions and visit counts
We have not set a time limit for these yet.

You can always ask us to delete information about you. See below.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we have stored about you, to have it corrected and to have it deleted. You can also object to our using it, and ask to have it handed over.

Write to tidemann@esbjugconsult.com. It costs nothing, and you get an answer within a month.

If you think we are doing something wrong, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet). Feel free to tell us first, and we will fix it.
