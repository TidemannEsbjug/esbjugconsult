FrostCopy Ireland · Licence

Enter your licence key

FrostCopy is free for private individuals, and free for organisations for 30 days. After that the organisation needs a licence. The program itself never connects to the internet.

  1. Open FrostCopy and click “Licence” at the top right. During the trial it says “Trial: … days left”.
  2. Paste the code from our email into the field. It starts with FROSTCOPYIE-. You can also double-click the licence file from the email, or drop it on the FrostCopy window.
  3. Click “Activate”. The licence covers the whole organisation: pass it on under “Share with colleagues”.
