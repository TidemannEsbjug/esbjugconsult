Sprout

The squat app where every rep grows something. Set the phone up, do your squats, and the camera counts them.

Support

Questions, bugs, or anything that isn't working: sproutappsupport@proton.me. Every message is read.

Common questions

It isn't counting my squats. Sprout needs to see all of you, standing, before it starts. Set the phone up two or three steps away so your head and your feet both fit in the picture. A rep counts when your hips drop to about knee height and you stand back up.

Where are the settings? Tap the small (i) in the bottom-left corner of the home screen. Sound, reminders and your account are all there.

How do I delete my account? Tap the (i), then Delete my account. Everything stored about you is removed straight away. You can also write to the address above and we will delete it for you.

Someone sent me something unkind. On a letter, tap Report this note or Block sender. In your friends list, tap the three dots beside their name. A blocked person can no longer see your garden or reach you.

My dew is gone after reinstalling. Dew and decorations are kept on your phone, so deleting the app removes them.
