Questions and answers

What is Sprout?

Sprout is an iPhone app that counts your squats with the camera. Every squat makes the day’s plant grow, and every day you train becomes a plant in your garden.

How does the app count squats?

Lean the phone against a wall, step two or three paces back so your whole body is in the picture, and squat. A squat counts when your hips drop to about knee height and you stand back up.

Am I being filmed?

No. The camera frames are analysed on your phone, one at a time, and discarded straight away. No video or photo is saved or sent.

What does Sprout cost?

The app is free, with no subscription and no ads. The only thing you can buy is dew, and you also earn dew by doing squats.

What is dew?

Dew is what you decorate your garden with: pots, stone walls, lights, new seeds and butterflies. You earn dew with every squat, and more of it the better your water is.

What are the water levels?

All the squats you have ever done improve your water, through twelve levels from Rain to Sunwater. Better water makes the stalk grow further with every squat. Your water never gets worse.

How do friends work?

Pick a name in Friends and add friends by searching for theirs. You can visit each other’s gardens and leave a butterfly with a few words. Friends is free.

What do I need to use the app?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is available in English, Norwegian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean. An Android version is in the works.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, not for counting. Squats are counted on the phone and your log is kept there. Friends needs a connection, because gardens and notes are shared through a server.

Can I change my daily goal?

Yes. The goal starts at 50 squats a day and you can set it to what suits you. The plant flowers when you reach it.

Does Sprout remind me to train?

Only if you want it to. You can turn on a daily reminder. It is scheduled by the phone itself, and we do not send push notifications.

Which exercises does Sprout count?

Bodyweight squats, and only those. The app is built around one exercise so that it can count it well.

How accurate is the counting?

It depends on the light, on how the phone is placed and on how you move. Sprout needs to see your whole body, from head to feet, before it starts. It can occasionally miss a squat or count one that was not there.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Tap the (i) on the home screen and choose Delete my account. Everything stored about you on the server is removed straight away. Your squat log stays on the phone until you clear it or delete the app.
