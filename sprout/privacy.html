Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Your squats stay on your phone. Sprout asks you to sign in when you first open it, which creates an account with us. Nothing else is sent to us until you take a name in Friends, the free part of the app where you and your friends can see each other's gardens. This page says exactly what that involves.

The camera

Sprout uses the front camera to count your squats. Frames are analysed on your device, one at a time, using Apple's on-device body-pose detection, and are discarded immediately.

No video or photo is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted. Nothing derived from the camera image leaves your phone. The camera runs only while the counting screen is open.

What stays on your phone

  • Your log: the date and number of squats in each set.
  • Your daily goal, kept where the home-screen widget can read it.
  • Your garden: the plants you chose, what you placed and shaped, and your dew.
  • Your settings: sound and reminders.

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. We do not send push notifications and have no way to.

Your account and Friends

Signing in stores the first item below. The rest is stored only once you take a name in Friends, so that it can be shown to the friends you accept:

  • Your account: signing in with Apple or Google gives us an account identifier and an email address. Apple lets you hide your real address behind a relay address. If you sign in with Google, Google also passes on the name and profile picture address of your Google account. We never show any of these to anyone, and Sprout does not use the name or the picture.
  • The name you pick, which other people use to find you.
  • Your garden as friends see it: how many squats you did on each day of the past year, today's count, your streak, your water level, your plant, and what you have placed and shaped.
  • Notes you write when you leave a butterfly in a friend's garden, and which butterfly it was.
  • Who your friends are, and friend requests waiting for an answer.
  • Touches on a friend's flower. The friend is never told who it was.
  • A random key for your phone, so that if we ever close your account we can show you a notice explaining why, even though you can no longer sign in. It identifies nothing else.
  • People you have blocked, so that a block follows your account to a new phone and a blocked person cannot ask to be your friend again. Only you can see this list.
  • Reports you send us about a person or a note. A report keeps the notes that person left in your garden, so that we can judge it fairly; they are removed from your garden at the same time.

Only people you have accepted as friends can see your garden and send you notes. Your squat log itself, set by set, is never uploaded.

This is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf. The database is in the European Union. We use it to run your account and Friends, and for nothing else.

Purchases

The only thing sold in Sprout is dew. Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Sprout does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not follow you across apps or websites. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

  • Your Friends account: in the app, tap the (i), then Delete my account. Your name, your shared garden, your friends and every note you wrote or received are removed from the server straight away.
  • Your log: Log, then Clear the whole log.
  • Everything on the phone: delete the app.

You can also write to us and we will delete your account for you.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to correct or delete it. Write to the address below. If you are in the EU or the UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Sprout is not aimed at children under 13, and Friends is not intended for them.

Changes

If what Sprout collects ever changes, this page will say so before that version ships.
