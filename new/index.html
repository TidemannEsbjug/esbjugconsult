Process

You see the site before you decide.

We build a working draft first. If you like it, we finish it.

  1. Call

    We look at your current site and what you want from it. You get a fixed price.

  2. Draft

    We build the front page with design, images and copy, and show it to you at its own address.

  3. Refine

    We change what you want changed and build the rest of the pages.

  4. Launch

    The site goes live on your domain. We keep an eye on it and help with changes afterwards.

FAQ

What people ask.

What does a new website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what they should do. After a short call you get a fixed price. It does not change along the way.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is designed and coded for the business it belongs to.

Where do the images come from?

We make them for you with tools like Midjourney and Grok Imagine, in a style that fits your business. If you have your own photos, we can use those instead or mix the two.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. We design for phones first, then for larger screens.

Can we keep our domain?

Yes. The new site goes live on the domain you already have, and your email is not affected.

What happens after launch?

We can look after hosting and changes for you. If you would rather have the files yourself, you get them.
