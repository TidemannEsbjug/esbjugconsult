Privacy Policy

4amachilles Studio · Effective 17 October 2026

4amachilles Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by Esbjug Consult, Norway ("we"). It publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account and other social accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

When the account holder authorizes the app, TikTok provides:

  • an access token and a refresh token, which let the app post on the account holder's behalf;
  • basic profile information (open ID and display name), used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • the status and ID of each post the app publishes.

The app requests only the user.info.basic and video.publish permissions. It does not read followers, messages, comments, analytics or other users' data.

2. How it is used

This data is used solely to publish videos the account holder has chosen to publish, at the times they choose, and to keep a log of what was posted. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. The posting log (post time, platform, post ID and link) is stored in a local database on the same computer. No data is kept on our servers.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok, as needed to publish the video through TikTok's API. We do not share it with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens stop working as soon as authorization is withdrawn, and are deleted from the Keychain when the app is disconnected. The account holder can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, or ask us to delete the data by email.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data about anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
